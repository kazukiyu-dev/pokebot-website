  • Separate party, PC, bag, wallet, and location for each region

Begin in minutes

How to start playing

Invite the bot

Add PokeBot to your Discord server using the official invite button.

Create your trainer

Run /start, enter your trainer name, and choose your first starter.

Explore and catch

Use /route info, /explore, and /wild to find your first encounters.

Challenge the League

Train your team, collect badges, defeat the Elite Four, and claim the Champion title.
